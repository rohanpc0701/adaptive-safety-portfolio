# Hua et al. 2024

**"Combining Cost-Constrained Runtime Monitors for AI Safety"**
arXiv 2507.15886

---

## Relevance

Closest prior work. Proposes offline, static cost-constrained combinations of safety monitors — the same cascade idea but without online adaptation.

**This project extends:** adds EXP3 online learning ([[EXP3 Bandit]]) and evaluates against an adaptive adversary ([[PAIR Attacker]]).

## Key difference

Hua et al.: offline optimization of a fixed policy.
This project: online adaptation to streaming data + adversarial robustness evaluation.
